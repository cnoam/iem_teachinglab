How to use the Databricks notebook

Serverless starts in seconds and shares a daily budget across your group — here's what to do differently because of that.

Cmd 1

Getting started

Open a notebook and attach it to Serverless from the compute dropdown, top-left. There's no cluster to wait for — it's ready in seconds.

Cmd 2

Detach when you're not using it

An attached notebook keeps using — and counts against your group's usage — even while it sits idle with nothing running. If you won't be actively working for the next few minutes, detach.

Detached
Costs nothing. Re-attaching takes seconds.

Don't count on it disconnecting itself — it may eventually happen, but there's no guaranteed timing. Walking away with a notebook attached can burn your group's budget for zero actual work.

Cmd 3

Your group's daily quota

Each group shares one compute-time budget per day — shared across everyone in the group, not per student. Several of you working at once, or notebooks left attached idle, all draw from the same total.

If your group goes over the limit, you're blocked from starting new work — running a new cell, opening a notebook — until the reset. Anything already running when the limit is hit keeps running; you just can't start anything else.

Resets at midnight. If you're blocked, there's nothing to fix or request — just wait for the reset, or plan your session around it.

Cmd 4

Quick checklist

  • Attach to Serverless, not a classic cluster.
  • Detach when you step away, even briefly.
  • Don't leave notebooks open overnight or between sessions.
  • Blocked? It resets at midnight — no need to contact anyone.
